Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your data from Furniture Sales Assistant, follow these steps:

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Send an email to tabishiqbal486@gmail.com with subject: "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include your Facebook Page name or ID in the email
  3. Your data will be deleted within 30 days

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Facebook Page access tokens
  • All post history associated with your account
  • Any stored customer conversation data

Alternatively

You can revoke the App's access to your Facebook account directly from Facebook:

  1. Go to Facebook → Settings → Security and Login → Apps and Websites
  2. Find "Furniture Sales Assistant" and click Remove
