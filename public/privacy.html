Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Furniture Sales Assistant ("the App") is operated by Tabish Iqbal for managing Facebook Page posts and customer interactions for a furniture business.

Information We Collect

The App collects and stores:

  • Facebook Page access tokens (to post on your behalf)
  • Product and catalogue information you enter
  • Customer enquiry messages from Facebook Messenger
  • Post history and scheduling data

How We Use Information

Information is used solely to operate the furniture sales automation features — publishing posts to your Facebook Pages and responding to customer enquiries.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties. Data is only shared with Meta (Facebook) as required to operate the Facebook integration.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely in a Supabase database. Facebook access tokens are stored encrypted.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by visiting our Data Deletion page or contacting us at tabishiqbal486@gmail.com.
